Turn scanning into a career.

01 / Product call

Keep the machinery. Replace the loop.

Programs already has pattern accounts, procedural signal fields, weighted rows, research gates, fleet scan stats, cooldowns, and cargo emission. The missing part is player agency.

Current proof of concept

  • Choose one of five pattern accounts.
  • Pay Repair Kits and roll immediately.
  • One SDU row per pattern.
  • Raw coordinate/time noise is hidden.
  • Linear power scaling is clipped by cargo.
  • Wait for cooldown.
→

Recommended release

  • Read Region signal climate.
  • Fit Scanner Charges, pattern, and cargo.
  • Detect one signal and commit its outcome.
  • Travel to recover before expiry.
  • Receive useful direct loot or Data Cubes.
  • Progress into new strategies and risk.
Loot-box ruling: no current authoritative gameplay source commits scanning to loot boxes. Data Cube cargo and scanning mappings are authored design intent. Ship cubes as tradeable research commodities first; evaluate earned-only containers after telemetry.

02 / Core loop

Seven verbs, one coherent profession.

The travel step turns scan stats into gameplay. Cooldown, target distance, cargo room, risk, and ship fit all become choices rather than numbers behind one button.

01 / READ

Read the map

Region risk, category climate, strength band, local exhaustion, and reachable patterns.

02 / FIT

Fit the fleet

Pattern, Scanner Charges, cargo room, scanner config, route fuel, and research.

03 / DETECT

Commit a scan

Cost sinks, future entropy is pinned, and one fleet signal is created.

04 / INTERPRET

Read the trace

Category, rarity band, distance, radius, expiry, and risk—not the exact item.

05 / MOVE

Take the route

Travel creates exposure, time pressure, fuel cost, and specialist value.

06 / RECOVER

Reveal the find

Verify location, reveal committed loot, settle cargo and full career XP.

07 / REINVEST

Analyze or trade

Use components and cubes for research, fitting, crafting, or the market.

03 / Pattern identities

Click a pattern to inspect its release identity and proposal tuning. Percentages are successful-recovery rarity mix, not current runtime odds.

06 / Data Runner journey

Every phase changes the player's decisions.

The existing tree already has the right branches. Stagger pattern unlocks instead of releasing all five together behind Signal Refinement.

L0–3

Surveyor

Broad Spectrum, Freelance Data Runner, Data Cube Analysis, and Regional Tiers. Learn the loop with reliable common loot.

L5–8

Field Analyst

Contested Space, Rare Cube Refinement, Sensor Mastery, and Focused Array. First real specialization.

L10–15

Signal Specialist

Amplification I, Rapid Cycling I, Epic Cube Refinement, and Enhanced Signal.

L18–25

Deep-Space Runner

High-risk and enemy-faction access, Amplification II, Rapid Cycling II, Legendary Cubes, and Deep Resonance.

L30–38

Anomaly Hunter

Deep Signal Refinement, final power/cadence nodes, Anomalous Cubes, and Spatial Anomaly.

L40

Master Data Runner

Capstone requiring cube, Region, signal-quality, amplification, cadence, and cross-career mastery.

Fast identity. Long mastery.

07 / Integrity

Exploration without a reroll machine.

The reward must be unknowable when cost is paid, fixed before the player chooses recovery, and bounded across fleet size and cargo.

One signal per fleet

No scan carousel while ignoring recoveries.

Future-hash reveal

The deciding entropy does not exist when the cost is committed.

Outcome fixed at commit

Abandoning cannot reroll into something better.

Recovery settlement

Full loot and most XP require arrival at the target.

Regional exhaustion

Repeated local recovery lowers rare opportunity; movement restores it.

Bounded scaling

Fixed discrete drops; square-root bulk multiplier capped at 2.25×.

Nonzero cost floor

Specialists are efficient, never free.

Capacity preflight

Warn before a valuable result can be clipped by cargo.

08 / Release scope

Five coordinated workstreams. Nineteen to twenty-six AI-days.

This is a real career loop and requires Programs, SES, economy, and FC work. A smaller immediate-loot build is a fallback, not the same product.
